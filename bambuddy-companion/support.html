Common questions

How do I connect the app to my Bambuddy server?

Open the app and enter your Bambuddy server URL (e.g. https://bambuddy.local). Sign in with your Bambuddy username and password. The app connects directly to your self-hosted instance.

The app can't reach my server

Make sure your phone is on the same network as your Bambuddy server (or that your server is accessible remotely). Check that the URL is correct and the server is running.

Which printers are supported?

Any printer supported by your Bambuddy server — including Bambu Lab X1C, X1E, P1S, P1P, A1, and A1 Mini.

Is there email support?

No. All support is handled through GitHub Issues and Discussions so conversations are public and searchable for everyone.
